# How to generate buyer enquiries with a genuinely curated property list

“Properties in [Suburb]” is a portal query. A useful custom list explains the brief, adds context a search box does not and names the trade-offs to verify.

## A concrete synthetic example

Brief: three-bedroom, single-level homes near [Suburb] shops, with outdoor upkeep described honestly. Fictional teaching rows:

- 4 Example Street: small courtyard, near the stated bus route; verify availability and upkeep.
- Unit 2, Sample Walk: lift access noted in the source; verify strata costs and lift operation.
- 9 Placeholder Road: larger yard; explain that upkeep may be higher.

These are not real listings or market evidence. Download the [curated-list template](/guides/resources/custom-list-facebook-ad-buyer-leads/curated-list-template.csv).

## Build and source it

Record the location context, property type, price, why it is included, one trade-off, source, permission check and last-checked date. Use agency-owned or authorised data and images. Do not claim exclusivity, availability or a valuation without proof.

The [form and delivery checklist](/guides/resources/custom-list-facebook-ad-buyer-leads/form-delivery-checklist.txt) covers the privacy link, source permissions, mobile link, phone purpose and separate marketing consent.

## Form and delivery copy

Use full name and email. Ask for mobile only after explaining why the agency may call. Keep optional marketing consent separate:

> Optional: Yes, send me occasional matching property updates from [Agency]. I can unsubscribe at any time.

Thank-you copy should open the actual list, show the last-checked date and state that curation is not a promise that every property is available. Download the [copy specimens](/guides/resources/custom-list-facebook-ad-buyer-leads/copy-specimens.txt).

## Meta test and review

Choose a spend that fits your economics; no fixed budget guarantees data. Check current account country, markets reached and Special Ad Category requirements. Rules may apply when an ad is based in or reaches the US, Canada or Europe. Australian domestic campaigns are not universally subject to one radius requirement. Nondiscrimination and truthful presentation apply everywhere.

Meta says delivery may stabilise around 50 results in a week after the last significant edit. Treat this as a platform learning reference, not a fixed three-day learning rule, guaranteed result or reason to tinker blindly. Review observed volume, valid contacts, useful conversations and cost after your named window.

A higher-intent form review step can improve the chance that details are checked, but it does not guarantee contactability or buyer intent.

## FAQs

### What makes a custom list more useful than a portal search?

Curate around a real brief, add context, show trade-offs and update it when the source changes. Do not call a lightly filtered results page exclusive.

### Do I need Meta’s Housing Special Ad Category?

Check current requirements for the account country and markets reached. Special rules may apply to housing-related ads, especially when based in or reaching the US, Canada or Europe.

### Can I target a particular neighbourhood or postcode?

Use only location controls currently available for your account and market. Do not promise postcode-level delivery or assume a fixed radius.

### How should I judge a small list or budget?

Do not invent a minimum property count or promise leads from a fixed budget. Pause when the list is too thin, document observed volume and economics, then revise one meaningful input at a time.
