# Stop showing every homeowner the same appraisal ad

The appraisal is not a bad offer. It is a high-commitment first step. A homeowner checking recent sales may not be ready to share an address, answer a sales call or discuss a move.

A practical ladder starts with two complete offers:

1. **Local evidence:** a source-noted sold-price list for a defined property type and suburb.
2. **Appraisal conversation:** a clear invitation for an active seller to discuss preparation, timing and evidence.

These are entry points, not a guaranteed sequence. Use the number of offers your budget and team can review well.

## Housing and geography

Meta’s published restrictions cover US-based advertisers and housing ads reaching the US, Canada or Europe. An Australian business advertising only in Australia is not automatically subject to those same audience restrictions; verify the current account controls and markets reached. Australia is not a universal radius-or-location rule. Check account country and current markets before launch, and apply nondiscrimination principles throughout.

See [Meta’s housing guidance](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/marketing-api/audiences/special-ad-category/).

## Two complete illustrative ads

### Offer 1 — local evidence

**Headline:** What did three-bedroom Como homes sell for this quarter?

**Body:** See three recent comparable sales, with sale dates, property type and source notes. No valuation is implied.

**CTA:** Get the local sales list.

**Form:** Full name, email, “Do you own a home of this type in Como?”, and express consent for the promised follow-up.

**Follow-up message:** “Here is the source-noted sales list you requested. Was there a particular result or property type you were comparing?” Do not open with “When are you selling?”

### Offer 2 — appraisal conversation

**Headline:** Planning to sell your Como home this year?

**Body:** Talk through likely preparation, timing and comparable evidence with an agent. The conversation is not a promise to list.

**CTA:** Request a property conversation.

**Form:** Full name, email, phone, property address, preferred contact time and consent.

**Follow-up message:** “Thanks for requesting a property conversation. We will cover preparation, timing and comparable evidence; there is no obligation to list. Is [time] still suitable?”

These are illustrative ads, not a performed case or universal template. Replace every local claim with evidence you can verify and use.

## Match the form and follow-up

Ask only for what the next step needs. Explain what happens after submission, include a privacy notice and collect express consent before promising recurring messages. Keep sender identity and unsubscribe handling appropriate to your market.

For the evidence-led version, read [How to use a sold-price list to earn seller leads](/guides/sold-price-list-seller-leads).

### Copyable delivery email

Subject: Your Como sales list

Hi [first name],
Here is the source-noted sales list you requested: [link]. It covers [period and property type], updated [date]. It is market context, not a valuation of your home. Reply if you want help comparing a result. Receiving this list does not sign you up for recurring updates.

[name] | [agency] | [contact details] | [privacy link]

### Appraisal confirmation

Hi [first name], thanks for requesting a property conversation with [agency]. Is [date/time] suitable for a call from [number]? We will discuss your questions, preparation, timing and comparable evidence. There is no obligation to list. Reply to change the time or cancel. [agent]

### Separate optional marketing permission

Optional: Yes, email me occasional Como market updates from [Agency]. This is separate from my resource or appointment request. I can unsubscribe at any time.

## A conservative planning example

Suppose you can spend A$600 over 14 days. One conservative starting hypothesis is A$360 on local evidence and A$240 on the appraisal conversation. This is a decision-based example, not a universal ratio.

Review delivery, contactability and downstream quality together. Do not call either offer a winner before it has enough delivery for a fair comparison.

## Frequently asked questions

### Should I stop running appraisal ads entirely?

No. Keep the appraisal for active sellers; make it one entry point alongside lower-commitment evidence and education offers.

### How many offers should I start with?

Start with two complete offers: local evidence and an appraisal invitation. Add another only when its form, follow-up and review owner are ready.

### Will lower-commitment offers attract unqualified people?

Some will. Use one useful qualifying question, then judge the offer by contactable and progressing leads rather than raw form volume.

### Do people move through the offers in order?

Not necessarily. The offers are entry points, not a guaranteed sequence. Keep geography, consent and follow-up clear at every stage.
