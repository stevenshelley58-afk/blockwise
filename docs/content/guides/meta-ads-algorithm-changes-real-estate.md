# How Meta's 2026 ad algorithm changes real estate advertising

Most explanations of Meta's advertising system begin and end with Andromeda.

That is now incomplete.

Meta replaced three layers of its ad system in roughly twelve months — the retrieval engine, the shared knowledge model, and the real-time ranking process. Each change shifts more of the matching work away from the targeting panel and toward the creative itself.

For real estate agents, the practical consequence is the same: the audience settings you spent years perfecting matter less than the ads you feed the system.

## The three layers

### Andromeda — retrieval

[Andromeda](https://engineering.fb.com/2024/12/02/production-engineering/meta-andromeda-advantage-automation-next-gen-personalized-ads-retrieval-engine/) is Meta's ads retrieval engine, introduced in December 2024 and fully rolled out across most objectives and placements by October 2025. Before Andromeda, the system retrieved ads using relatively narrow signals. Now it scans tens of millions of eligible ads and reduces them to a few thousand candidates using a co-designed hardware-software-machine-learning system built on NVIDIA Grace Hopper Superchips and Meta's own MTIA accelerators.

Meta reports a 6% improvement in retrieval recall and an 8% improvement in ad quality in selected segments. Those are platform-level figures, not guarantees for any individual advertiser — but at Meta's scale, single-digit improvements represent meaningful change.

The key shift: Andromeda reads your creative — format, opening seconds, tone, subject matter — and predicts which user is most likely to respond. Your audience inputs become suggestions, not constraints.

### GEM — shared knowledge

[GEM](https://engineering.fb.com/2025/11/10/ml-applications/metas-generative-ads-model-gem-the-central-brain-accelerating-ads-recommendation-ai-innovation/) (Generative Ads Recommendation Model) is a foundation model that transfers learning across Meta's entire fleet of advertising models. It launched in early 2025 and has been running in the backend for all advertisers since Q2 2025, with no opt-in required.

Meta reports that GEM delivered a 5% increase in Instagram ad conversions and a 3% increase on Facebook Feed during Q2. Again, these are Meta-reported platform averages.

For advertisers, the practical effect is that Meta's models are getting better at understanding nuanced behavioural patterns — when a user engages with video on Instagram but scrolls past it on Facebook, for example — and adjusting delivery accordingly.

### Adaptive Ranking — real-time complexity

In March 2026, Meta announced its [Adaptive Ranking Model](https://engineering.fb.com/2026/03/31/ml-applications/meta-adaptive-ranking-model-bending-the-inference-scaling-curve-to-serve-llm-scale-models-for-ads/), which brings LLM-scale model complexity into the real-time ad ranking process. Meta reports a 3% increase in ad conversions and a 5% increase in click-through rate for targeted users.

The model serves trillion-parameter models at sub-second latency. In plain terms: the system that decides which ad to show is now dramatically more capable of identifying relationships between a person's behaviour, an advertiser's objective, and the content of an ad.

### Sequence learning — behavioural context

Meta also introduced [sequence learning](https://engineering.fb.com/2024/11/19/data-infrastructure/sequence-learning-personalized-ads-recommendations/) in late 2024, which examines patterns across a person's previous interactions rather than relying solely on manually constructed targeting signals. Meta reports 2% to 4% more conversions in selected segments.

## The PACE framework

Use four checks before you build your next campaign.

| Check | What to decide | Good example | Warning sign |
|---|---|---|---|
| **P: Problem diversity** | Do your ads address different homeowner problems, or the same problem reworded? | Sold-price report + renovate-or-sell guide + buyer-demand update | Three appraisal ads with different headline wording |
| **A: Audience as suggestion** | Are you treating location as a constraint and leaving the rest to Meta? | Suburb targeting + Advantage+ audience expansion | Seven interest filters stacked inside one postcode |
| **C: Creative as signal** | Does each ad contain enough specific information for the algorithm to match it? | "Four-bedroom homes in Baldivis — see recent comparable sales" | "Perth homeowners: find out what your property is worth" |
| **E: Evidence over assertion** | Does each ad demonstrate local knowledge with verifiable proof? | Three recent sales with dates and property types | "Local market expert" with no supporting evidence |

If one of these checks is weak, fix it before spending.

## 1. Stop over-targeting

The old approach was to build a precise audience: an age range, several property-related interests, a postcode and an income proxy, then show everyone in that audience the same appraisal ad.

The new system makes that approach counterproductive.

[Advantage+ audience](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/273363992030035) can use your audience inputs as suggestions and search more broadly when it predicts that doing so will improve performance. Advantage+ is now the default for eligible leads campaigns — you have to actively turn parts of it off, not on.

An interest in home renovation could represent a homeowner planning to sell, a renter decorating, a tradesperson, an architecture student, or someone who watches renovation programs. Detailed targeting creates the feeling of precision without proving the audience contains likely sellers.

### What to keep

Your location and compliance settings still define where Meta is allowed to operate. An agent servicing Perth's southern corridor cannot effectively service every homeowner in Australia.

Keep these constraints:

- A defined geographic service area — a suburb, a group of adjoining suburbs, or a local government area
- The [Special Ad Category: Housing](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/1198401317374558) declaration, which is required for real estate advertising
- Exclusions where commercially or legally necessary

Remove everything else. Let the creative do the narrowing.

## 2. Give Meta different problems, not different colours

Changing the background colour on an appraisal graphic does not create another targeting option. Changing the homeowner's problem does.

A sold-price report, a buyer-demand update, a downsizing guide and an appraisal invitation are four different reasons to engage. Meta can match those reasons to different people, even if they live in the same suburb.

| Ad concept | Homeowner situation | What the ad offers | Next step |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sold-price report | Curious owner checking the market | Recent comparable sales list | Download |
| Buyer-demand update | Owner considering timing | What buyers are paying more for | View summary |
| Renovate-or-sell guide | Preparing seller | Pre-sale decision guide | Download |
| Appraisal invitation | Active seller | Property strategy meeting | Book a call |

These ads still support the same commercial objective — more listings. They simply approach it through different homeowner situations.

## 3. Broad audience, narrow message

An ad can be delivered broadly within a suburb while speaking narrowly:

> Own an older four-bedroom home in Leeming and unsure whether renovating before sale will pay off?

The audience settings may not explicitly identify that exact owner. The message does. Someone who does not recognise the situation scrolls past. Someone who does stops, reads and engages.

This is voluntary self-selection, and it is how the current system is designed to work. Broad delivery is not permission to be generic — it is the reason the message must be specific.

## 4. Run separate ads for separate situations

Within the same geographic market, run distinct messages:

- Apartment owners concerned about competing listings
- Family-home owners considering an upgrade
- Landlords reviewing an investment
- Downsizers deciding whether to move
- Owners preparing a deceased estate
- Homeowners comparing renovation with an immediate sale

Do not combine all six into one ad. Each situation should have its own argument, proof and next step. The broader Meta's delivery system becomes, the more specific each message must become.

## 5. Keep the delivery structure controlled

Meta increasingly recommends automated audience, budget and placement settings. [Advantage+ leads campaigns](https://www.facebook.com/business/ads/meta-advantage-plus/leads) can automate audience, placements and campaign budget.

This is not the same as advertising without boundaries. It means the boundaries should reflect genuine business constraints — where you can service a listing — rather than assumptions about which interests predict a future seller.

A practical setup:

- **Objective:** Leads
- **Special Ad Category:** Housing
- **Audience:** Your service area, without unnecessary interest stacking
- **Placements:** Advantage+ placements unless you have evidence for a specific exclusion
- **Budget:** A daily amount you can sustain for a full test window (14 days minimum)

## 6. Track what happens after the lead arrives

Meta can only optimise toward the outcomes it receives. If the only signal is "form submitted," the system searches for more people likely to submit forms — not more people likely to become listings.

If your CRM can send downstream outcomes back to Meta through the [Conversions API for CRM](https://www.facebook.com/business/generate-leads/conversions-api-for-crm), the system can optimise toward leads more likely to become qualified. Meta reports that advertisers using this setup saw an average 15% reduction in cost per quality lead and a 44% increase in the rate at which leads became quality leads, compared with campaigns optimised only for lead submissions.

These are Meta-reported averages, not guaranteed results. The point is that feedback changes what the system optimises for.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I still need to set a location target?

Yes. Your geographic service area is a genuine business constraint, and Meta's system respects it. What you should remove is the layering of speculative interests inside that location. Let the creative identify the homeowner situation; let the location setting define where you can operate.

### Does this mean I should run 50 ads at once?

No. A local agent spending a modest daily budget should not blindly upload dozens of ads. Start with six to twelve genuinely distinct assets — different problems, different proofs, different offers — and let the system learn which messages create real conversations.

### Is "creative is the new targeting" just a marketing phrase?

It reflects a real architectural change. Andromeda retrieves ads by reading creative signals. Advantage+ audience treats your audience inputs as suggestions. The content of your ad now plays a larger role in determining who responds and who Meta continues showing it to. Audience settings have not disappeared — they have been demoted.

### Should I select the Housing Special Ad Category for real estate ads?

Yes, when the campaign relates to housing. Meta requires advertisers to select the relevant Special Ad Category, and failing to do so may result in ad rejection. The algorithm changes do not remove compliance obligations — they make strong creative even more important because you cannot depend on unrestricted micro-targeting.
