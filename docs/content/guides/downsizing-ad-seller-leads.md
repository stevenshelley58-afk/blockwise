# How to test a transparent downsizer campaign for seller intent

The hypothesis is simple: some people seeking easier upkeep, accessible layouts, a different lifestyle or a smaller financial commitment already own a home and may need to sell before buying. Test that hypothesis openly. Do not disguise a seller pitch as a buyer registration.

## Make the buyer offer real

Define the brief in terms a buyer can check: single-level homes near services, compact gardens, lift access or another genuine local need. Show trade-offs such as stairs, strata costs, parking, distance and maintenance. “Low maintenance” does not mean “no maintenance”.

Use only properties and images you are permitted to share. If the list has no matching stock, pause, broaden the clearly stated brief, or choose another angle. Do not invent properties or send people to an empty page.

## Transparent scripts

Start as a buyer-service conversation:

> Hi [name], it’s [agent] from [agency]. You requested the [list], so I’m checking that the link worked. What would make a home easier for you to live in or maintain?

Then ask:

> Do you currently own a home, rent, or prefer not to say?

If they own one:

> Would you need to sell before buying, or are you exploring options only? I can explain the sale process if useful; there is no obligation.

Record the answer and preference. Some people will have another agent or will not want a sale discussion.

## Campaign and geographic scope

Use a genuine list, a short form and a named review date. Choose a spend that fits your economics; A$20 daily for 14 days is only a planning example. Meta Special Ad Category restrictions can apply when an ad is based in or reaches the US, Canada or Europe. Australian domestic campaigns are not universally subject to one radius rule. Check the current account requirements and apply nondiscrimination everywhere.

## Two review windows

The first 14 days are for link health, consent, contactability and observed quality. They are not proof of seller outcomes.

Keep that cohort together for 90 days and track valid contacts, homeowners, seller conversations, appraisals and listings. A click or one form submission does not prove urgency or shorten a selling timeline.

## FAQs

### Is this deceptive if I want seller leads?

It is transparent only when the ad offers a genuine buyer resource, delivers it, identifies the agent and explains the reason for follow-up. Ask about ownership and a possible sale in the conversation; do not hide a seller pitch behind a false registration.

### What percentage of downsizer leads will be sellers?

There is no universal percentage. Measure your own funnel with each rate using the preceding stage as its denominator.

### What if there is little or no suitable stock?

Do not publish a thin or fictional list. Pause, revise the stated brief, use a clearly explained waitlist, or choose another campaign angle.

### How long should I track seller outcomes?

Review quality after 14 days and track the 90-day cohort for conversations, appraisals and listings. A click is not evidence of a shortened timeline.
