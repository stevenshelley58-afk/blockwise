# Blockwise instructions

Follow `AGENTS.md` as the source of truth. Blockwise is the customer product
over Frank-built layered template packs; Frank generation and Hermes research
are separate systems. Do not restore or document the deleted flat-clone path.

Production work runs through the VPS checkout and controlled Caddy target.

localhost or Vercel Preview as release acceptance. For operational status,
health, provenance, migration, and rollback use [docs/README.md](docs/README.md)
and its linked current runbooks.
