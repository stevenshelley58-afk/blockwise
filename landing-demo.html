FAQ

Questions agents ask us.

Do I need to understand ads? +

No. That's the whole point. We build, launch and manage everything. You don't need to know a thing about how ads work — you just approve what we propose and follow up the leads that come in.

Do I approve what runs? +

Always. Nothing goes live until you've seen it and said yes. You can ask for changes to the wording, the look or the budget before anything starts.

Can I see where the money goes? +

Yes — to the dollar. Your dashboard shows exactly how much has been spent and how much is left, updated as the campaign runs. No guessing, no waiting for a report.

Is this for seller leads or buyer leads? +

Sellers. Blockwise is built to get you in front of homeowners who are thinking about selling, so you win more listings — not just collect buyer enquiries.

Is this an agency? +

Not in the usual sense. There's no account manager to chase and no vague monthly report. It's a managed system: we do the work behind the scenes, and you see the leads and results yourself, any time.
