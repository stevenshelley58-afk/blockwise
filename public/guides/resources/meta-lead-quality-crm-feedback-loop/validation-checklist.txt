CRM feedback validation checklist

[ ] Every lead is deduplicated and mapped to the correct ad account.
[ ] Lead ID is captured where available; it is recommended, not the only possible identifier.
[ ] Event names and timestamps come from the CRM record, not a guess.
[ ] Qualification rules are written, repeatable and reviewed for bias.
[ ] Consent, notice, access and retention rules are documented for the market.
[ ] Only approved fields are sent; never passwords, tokens, free-text notes or secrets.
[ ] Test events are visible in the selected dataset and errors are triaged.
[ ] Reconcile CRM counts with Meta diagnostics before choosing an optimisation stage.
