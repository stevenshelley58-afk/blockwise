Learn more: campaign structure & naming
Why one campaign per goal? Meta's delivery system (Andromeda) learns from every dollar spent. Splitting the same audience across several campaigns fragments that learning, so each one stays in the learning phase longer and costs more per lead.
  • Consolidate: combine similar ad sets instead of creating new ones — Meta's own guidance.
  • Learning phase: an ad set needs ~50 optimisation events in the week after its last significant edit to exit learning.
  • Significant edits reset learning: targeting, creative, optimisation event, adding a new ad, pausing 7+ days, or a big budget jump.
Naming pattern: [Market]_[Objective]_[Theme]_[Period] — e.g. WA_SellerLeads_Winter_Jul26. It keeps your account scannable when you have ten campaigns next winter. Tips & education: why 3–5 creatives?
In roughly the first 7–12 days, Meta tests your ads with different people and placements. After that learning phase, most of your budget flows to the ad performing best — the others keep running lightly so Meta can keep comparing.
  • More creatives = more for Meta to test, and a better chance of a clear winner.
  • Mix formats (video + static) — they win in different placements.
  • Refreshing creative every 4–6 weeks fights fatigue without resetting everything at once.

Learn more: picking an objective
Your objective tells Meta's delivery system which outcome to optimise for — it's the single biggest lever on results, and it's a significant edit if changed on a live campaign.
  • Leads is right when you want names & phone numbers (appraisals, vendor packs).
  • Traffic is right for awareness — clicks, not enquiries.
  • Keep one objective per campaign. Two campaigns with the same audience but different objectives compete against each other in auction.

Learn more: budget & the learning phase
  • Rule of thumb: daily budget ≥ 5× your target cost per lead, so the ad set can hit ~50 results a week and exit learning. At ~$4/lead that's ≈ $20/day.
  • Run 7+ days before judging — Meta explicitly says don't evaluate CPA in days 1–3.
  • Small budget tweaks are fine ($100 → $101). Big jumps ($100 → $1,000) can restart learning.
  • "Run until closed" suits always-on seller lead flow; pause from Results any time.
