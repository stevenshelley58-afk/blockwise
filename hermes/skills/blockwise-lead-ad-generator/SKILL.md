# blockwise-lead-ad-generator

## Purpose

Turn the article into a draft Meta lead generation ad package for Blockwise
agent acquisition.

## Output

Strict JSON with campaign brief, five ad variants, instant form placeholder,
lead scoring placeholder, and compliance notes.

## Constraints

- Draft-only.
- No live Meta campaign creation.
- Do not imply guaranteed leads, appraisals, or listings.

## Tools

- `blockwise.prompt_registry.load`
- `blockwise.model_router.route`
