# blockwise-social-post-generator

## Purpose

Create organic Facebook and Instagram drafts that point back to the article or
lead magnet.

## Output

Strict JSON with Facebook post, Instagram post, Instagram story frames, and
optional LinkedIn copy.

## Constraints

- Avoid fake guarantees, generic hashtag stuffing, and excessive emojis.
- No social post is published without approval.

## Tools

- `blockwise.prompt_registry.load`
- `blockwise.model_router.route`
